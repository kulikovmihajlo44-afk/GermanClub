01 / About

More than a language class.

Deutsch-Klub is a student-run community for cultural immersion and authentic engagement with the German-speaking world.

We go beyond textbook grammar — our sessions are living conversations, guided debates, film screenings, and literary discussions that place language in its true cultural context.

Whether you are practising spoken German for travel, preparing for a semester abroad, or developing academic communication skills, our peer-led workshops and faculty partnerships offer the structure and challenge you need.

All levels are welcome. What unites us is curiosity — about language, history, architecture, literature, and the culture German opens up.

03 / Levels

Find your starting point.

A0

Absolute Beginner

No prior knowledge required. Start from zero — the alphabet, greetings, and essential everyday phrases.

A1

Elementary

Foundational vocabulary and simple sentence structures. Hold basic conversations and introduce yourself confidently.

B2

Upper Intermediate

Engage with complex texts and native speakers. Articulate ideas fluently on a wide range of topics.

C1

Proficient

Near-native command. Produce well-structured, detailed texts and understand demanding implicit meaning.

04 / Workflow

Three steps. No guesswork.

step_01

Measure

A free adaptive placement test: 20 questions that get harder or easier with every answer. Your level, A0 to C1, in about 10 minutes.

→ /test
step_02

Practise

Your own plan in the app: grammar lessons with exercises and flashcards scheduled by the FSRS algorithm, ten new words a day.

→ /app
step_03

Speak

Every Sunday the club meets in person: conversations, debates and films with people at every level.
